# Portfolio content guide

This site separates verified accomplishments, active work, and future research direction. Do not convert an aspiration into an accomplishment, and do not publish confidential health, employer, client, or patient information.

## Project entry

Add summary metadata to `_data/projects.yml`, create a matching page under `projects/`, and use this order:

1. **Problem** — the real-world or technical gap.
2. **Research question** — the testable question guiding the work.
3. **Healthcare context** — why the problem matters, without overstating clinical use.
4. **Data or inputs** — sources, access constraints, governance, and preprocessing.
5. **Method** — architecture, baselines, and design choices.
6. **Evaluation** — metrics, test design, subgroup analysis, and failure cases.
7. **Results** — only measured results, with enough context to interpret them.
8. **Limitations** — data, method, validity, and deployment constraints.
9. **Future work** — concrete next questions or experiments.
10. **Artifacts** — repository, report, demo, or citation when public.

Use one of these status labels consistently: `Research exploration`, `Prototype`, `Ongoing`, `Production`, or `Completed`.

## Publication or writing entry

Record title, complete author list, venue or publisher, year, status, and a stable public link. Include DOI or arXiv identifier only when one exists. Keep peer-reviewed papers, preprints, technical reports, articles, and talks in separate sections.

## Experience entry

Include organization, title, dates, and two to four contributions. Emphasize the problem, technical or research responsibility, evaluation, and outcome. Use quantitative claims only when they are verified and publishable.

## Style

- Prefer specific verbs and short paragraphs.
- Define uncommon abbreviations on first use.
- Distinguish `designed`, `implemented`, `evaluated`, and `deployed`.
- State when evaluation is planned or results are not yet available.
- Use descriptive link text and meaningful image alt text.
