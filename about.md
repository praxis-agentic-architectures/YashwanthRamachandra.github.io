I am an AI and machine learning practitioner developing a research focus at the intersection of artificial intelligence, healthcare, and real-world clinical systems. My current technical work explores generative and agentic AI patterns—including retrieval, structured outputs, tool use, and workflow orchestration—with the longer-term aim of studying trustworthy clinical AI.

My interests include multimodal learning across imaging, clinical text, and structured health data; retrieval-augmented reasoning; clinical decision support; and evaluation methods that make safety, uncertainty, and limitations visible. I am particularly interested in the difficult translation layer between a promising model and a system that people can responsibly use.

The direction of this portfolio is intentionally evidence-led. Completed work, research questions, evaluation plans, and future goals are labeled separately. As the work develops, this site will become a record of reproducible experiments, technical writing, and research contributions.

## Working values

- **Rigor:** connect claims to methods and evidence.
- **Reliability:** study failure modes, not only successful demonstrations.
- **Transparency:** document assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations.
- **Clinical relevance:** treat workflow and human oversight as core design inputs.
- **Open practice:** share reusable technical artifacts when data and governance permit.
